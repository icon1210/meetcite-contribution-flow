## 4. Note on Global Talent

The Global Talent route (digital technology) looks for evidence of **exceptional** talent or promise, usually shown
through recognition outside one's own work: impact, adoption, independent validation and letters from established
experts. A university group project built with AI coding assistance is **not enough on its own**.

Right now, the honest strengths here are:
* product leadership;
* end-to-end delivery and launch;
* responsible AI design (evidence-grounded outputs);
* transparent use of AI.

The gaps are:
* real users;
* measured impact;
* independent recognition;
* personal technical contributions that can be shown separately from AI output.

The table in section 3 shows legitimate ways to close those gaps over time. Applicants should check the current
criteria with the endorsing body and seek independent advice.
